How to Use

  1. Flash the Receiver: Click the "INSTALL RECEIVER FIRMWARE" button to flash the ground station board.
  2. After flashing, connect the Receiver board to this computer via USB.
  3. View Data: Click the "CONNECT" button in the "Live Data" section to start receiving data.
  4. The live signal quality and decoded packets from your CanSat will appear below.
