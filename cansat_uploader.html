How to Use

  1. Flash the Board: Click the "INSTALL FIRMWARE" button below to flash your CanSat board.
  2. After flashing is complete, press the RESET/EN button on your ESP32.
  3. Connect: Click the "CONNECT" button in the "Live CanSat Data" section.
  4. After your session, click "DISCONNECT", then "DOWNLOAD LOG" to save the data.
